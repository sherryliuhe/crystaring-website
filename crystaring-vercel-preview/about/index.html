About Crystaring Light

China-based OEM/ODM manufacturing support for glass lamp shades and lighting glass components.

Company overview

Crystaring Light / Zhongshan Crystaring Light Technology Co., Ltd. is a China-based manufacturer of glass lamp shades and lighting glass components with 25 years of manufacturing experience. Based in Zhongshan, Guangdong, China, Crystaring supports overseas lighting brands, manufacturers, importers and distributors with OEM/ODM glass shade production, repeat component supply and custom glass development.

For more than two decades, Crystaring has supplied glass lighting components to demanding overseas markets, including Europe and North America. Our focus is not finished lighting retail, but specialist glass shade manufacturing for lighting companies that require stable quality, specification confirmation and export-ready supply.

What We Supply

Crystaring's main product series include opal mouth-blown glass shades, transparent mouth-blown glass shades, alabaster-effect glass shades and Pyrex-style / borosilicate glass components.

Lighting Applications

Products can be reviewed for pendant lights, wall lights, ceiling lights, outdoor lighting ranges, replacement glass shade requirements and other lighting glass component applications.

Who We Work With

We work with lighting brands, lighting manufacturers, importers, wholesalers, distributors, replacement glass shade suppliers, product managers, procurement managers and sourcing teams.

How We Support Buyers

Crystaring supports repeat catalogue parts, custom size and opening review, sample-based development, mould cost estimation, finish confirmation, packing discussion and export shipment coordination.

OEM / ODM Support

Specification confirmation before quotation

Buyers can send drawings, product photos, samples or existing glass references for feasibility review. For custom or replacement glass projects, we review whether existing moulds can be used or whether new mould development is required.

  • Final dimensions, openings, finish, packing, MOQ and lead time should be confirmed before quotation or production.
  • Custom sizes, openings, colours and finishes are available upon request and should be confirmed before quotation.
  • Glass shade only; electrical parts are not included unless specified.
