Capabilities

Manufacturing Support for Lighting Glass Components

Crystaring focuses on lighting glass component manufacturing rather than finished retail lighting. Our production support covers mouth-blown glass shades, opal glass, transparent glass, alabaster-effect glass, Pyrex-style / borosilicate glass components and selected custom glass requirements.

Mouth-Blown Glass Production

Mouth-blown glass production is used for many decorative lighting glass shade applications. Shapes, dimensions, openings and finishes are reviewed according to product drawings, samples or reference photos before quotation and production.

Opal, Transparent & Alabaster-Effect Glass

Our main glass shade ranges include opal mouth-blown glass, transparent glass and alabaster-effect glass. These products are commonly used for decorative lighting ranges, replacement glass shade requirements and OEM/ODM lighting projects.

Pyrex-style / Borosilicate Glass Components

Crystaring also supports Pyrex-style / borosilicate glass components for lighting applications where suitable glass material, structure and usage requirements are confirmed. Technical requirements, dimensions and application details should be reviewed before quotation.

Custom Development Based on Samples, Drawings or Photos

Buyers can provide drawings, product photos, existing samples or project specifications for review. Crystaring evaluates glass shape, opening, finish, mould requirements and production feasibility before preparing a quotation.

Mould & Existing Part Support

For repeat parts or replacement glass shade projects, buyers may provide existing samples or reference photos. We review whether an existing mould can be used or whether new mould development is required.

Quality Control & Defect Rejection

Quality is one of Crystaring's core strengths. Glass shades are checked before packing for visible defects, basic dimensions, openings, finish and order requirements according to the confirmed specification.

Packing & Export

Export Packing & Shipment Support

Because glass shades are fragile components, packing requirements are reviewed before shipment. Individual protection, inner boxes, master cartons, palletizing, labels and buyer-specific packing requirements can be discussed according to the order and shipping method.

  • Final appearance, dimensions and openings should be confirmed with samples before bulk production.
  • Defective pieces are rejected during production and inspection to support stable quality for overseas lighting customers.
  • Existing mould availability should be confirmed before quotation.
