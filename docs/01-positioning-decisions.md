# Positioning Decisions

## Frontend Narrow, Backend Broad

The public website should be narrow and clear: glass lamp shades and lighting glass components.

Behind the scenes, Crystaring may handle broader custom glass work, but the public-facing website should avoid looking unfocused. This protects B2B credibility and helps search engines understand the site.

## Why The Website Should Not Look Like A Finished Lighting Brand

Crystaring supplies glass components, not complete consumer lighting products. If the site looks like a finished lighting brand, buyers may misunderstand the business model and assume Crystaring sells complete fixtures.

This also creates risk around electrical testing, compliance, wiring and finished-light responsibility. Those claims should not be introduced unless Crystaring has verified them and wants to publicly support them.

## Why The Website Focuses On Lighting Glass Components

Lighting glass components are the strongest B2B category for the current site:

- It matches overseas buyer sourcing intent.
- It supports OEM/ODM enquiries.
- It avoids retail-style positioning.
- It keeps product claims conservative.
- It allows product pages to focus on images, model references and confirm-before-quotation language.

## Decorative Glass Vases

Decorative glass vases can be shown as a secondary product category. They should be presented as selected decorative glass items, not as the homepage's main positioning.

They should not dilute the main message:

OEM/ODM manufacturer and export supplier of glass lamp shades and lighting glass components.

## Forbidden Wording

Avoid reintroducing these public terms unless Crystaring later approves them:

- borosilicate
- sales@crystaring.com
- Glass Lighting Manufacturer
- Opal Glass Light
- finished lighting
- 01 Series / 02 Series / 03 Series / 04 Series

Also avoid invented or unverified claims about:

- MOQ
- lead time
- certifications
- production capacity
- factory size
- electrical compliance
- finished-light testing

## Approved Wording

Preferred wording includes:

- Glass Lamp Shades Manufacturer
- Glass lamp shades and lighting glass components
- OEM/ODM glass lamp shades
- Lighting glass components
- Pyrex-style glass components
- Confirm before quotation
- Final specifications confirmed before quotation and production
- Export-ready packing, when used conservatively
