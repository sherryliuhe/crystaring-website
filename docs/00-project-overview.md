## Core Public Positioning

Crystaring is positioned as an OEM/ODM manufacturer and export supplier of glass lamp shades and lighting glass components for overseas lighting brands, manufacturers, importers, wholesalers and distributors.

The website should communicate clearly that Crystaring supplies component-level glass products for lighting projects. It should not look like a retail lighting brand or a finished lighting fixture company.

## What Crystaring Is

- A supplier of glass lamp shades and lighting glass components.
- A partner for OEM/ODM lighting glass development.
- A source for repeat lighting glass component supply.
- A company with experience serving overseas B2B lighting customers.

## What Crystaring Is Not

- Not publicly positioned as a finished lighting manufacturer.
- Not positioned as an electrical lighting brand.
- Not positioned as a retail store.
- Not making public claims about unverified certifications, capacity, MOQ, lead time or factory size.

## Main Product Groups

- Opal mouth-blown glass shades
- Transparent mouth-blown glass shades
- Alabaster-effect glass shades
- Pyrex-style glass components
- Selected decorative glass vases as a secondary category

Decorative glass vases should remain secondary. They should not replace the main lighting glass component positioning.
