OEM/ODM

Custom glass shades for lighting programs

Crystaring supports OEM/ODM glass shade development for lighting brands, manufacturers and importers. Buyers can provide drawings, product photos, existing samples or target specifications for feasibility review before quotation.

Custom development workflow

01Send drawing / photo / sample

Share product references, project requirements or target dimensions.

02Feasibility review

Glass type, shape, opening and application requirements are reviewed.

03Specification confirmation

Size, opening, finish, colour and edge requirements are confirmed before quotation.

04Mould review or cost estimate

Existing mould availability or new mould cost is reviewed if needed.

05Sample production

Samples are prepared according to confirmed project requirements.

06Sample approval

Appearance, dimensions, finish and packing details are checked before bulk order.

07Bulk production

Production follows the confirmed sample and specification requirements.

08Packing and export shipment

Export packing is arranged according to product shape and shipping method.
