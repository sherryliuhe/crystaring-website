Capabilities

Manufacturing Capability

Crystaring supports overseas lighting brands, manufacturers and importers with mouth-blown glass shade production, Pyrex-style / borosilicate glass components, quality-focused production and export packing for fragile glass products.

Custom Development

Based on samples, drawings or photos

Glass shape, dimensions, openings, finish, mould requirements and project feasibility are reviewed before quotation.

Quality Control

Inspection before packing

Glass shades are checked for visible defects, basic dimensions, openings and finish before packing.

Mouth-Blown Glass Production

For decorative lighting glass shade applications where shape and finish need to match the buyer's product program.

Opal, Transparent & Alabaster-Effect Glass

Main shade ranges for pendant lights, wall lights, ceiling lights, outdoor ranges and replacement programs.

Pyrex-style / Borosilicate Glass Components

Reviewed for lighting applications where structure, dimensions and use requirements are suitable.

Mould & Existing Part Support

Existing mould availability is reviewed before quotation; new mould development may be required.

Packing & Export

Export Packing & Shipment Support

Because glass shades are fragile components, packing requirements are reviewed before shipment. Individual protection, inner boxes, master cartons, palletizing, labels and buyer-specific packing requirements can be discussed according to the order and shipping method.

  • Final appearance, dimensions and openings should be confirmed with samples before bulk production.
  • Defective pieces are rejected during production and inspection to support stable quality for overseas lighting customers.
  • Existing mould availability should be confirmed before quotation.
