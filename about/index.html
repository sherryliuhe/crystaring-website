About Crystaring Light

China-based OEM/ODM manufacturer and export supplier of glass lamp shades and lighting glass components.

Company overview

25 Years Manufacturing ExperienceCrystaring has supplied lighting glass components to overseas lighting brands, manufacturers and importers for more than two decades, including demanding European, American and Australian markets.

Crystaring Light / Zhongshan Crystaring Light Technology Co., Ltd. is a China-based OEM/ODM manufacturer and export supplier of glass lamp shades and lighting glass components. Based in Zhongshan, Guangdong, China, Crystaring has 25 years manufacturing experience in glass shade manufacturing, custom development, repeat component supply, quality control and export-ready packing for fragile glass products.

B2B Customers

Who We Work With

Crystaring works with established lighting brands, manufacturers, importers and distributors across Europe, the United States and Australia. Many of our customers require stable repeat supply, custom glass shade development, replacement glass parts and export-ready packing for demanding lighting product ranges.

What We Supply

Opal mouth-blown glass shades, transparent mouth-blown glass shades, alabaster-effect glass shades and Pyrex-style glass components.

Quality Control

inspection before packing

Quality is one of Crystaring's core strengths. Glass shades are checked before packing for visible defects, basic dimensions, openings, finish and order requirements according to the confirmed specification. Pieces with visible defects are rejected during production and inspection to support stable supply for overseas lighting customers.
